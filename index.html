Who We Are

The mission of Orphan Connect is to provide abundant love of God to orphaned and vulnerable children by equipping each child for a fulfilling, independent Christian life in their community. We restore hope for orphaned and vulnerable children so that every child will experience God’s love and know Jesus as Savior.

We also provide web-based education to orphaned and vulnerable children. We build a network via the satellite to provide Internet access and use the wireless network and laptop computers and tablets for each child to learn English and mathematics to overcome any disadvantages caused by missing parents and severe poverty.

What We Do

Provide Web-Based Training

  • Install High-speed, low latency broadband internet via Satellites
  • Provide Web Content for English & Mathematics
  • Install Robust Wireless Local Area Network
  • Provide Laptops & Tablets.

Orphan Connect aims to connect orphans and vulnerable children all over the world with free web-based education. Many children around the world do not have access to learning because of lacking resources and a connection to the Internet. Orphan Connect exists to change that.

The Technology

One Remote Place at a Time

The main barrier to the effective use of teaching materials is the technology (for example, poor access, slower downloading) rather than the design of the learning materials themselves. It is vital that teachers and children need expert help with technical issues in the planning, design, and delivery of web-based learning programs. Orphan Connect provides the best-in-class technology for orphans in remote locations to be able to learn and have a dream for the future.

Core Values

Helping The Community

Jesus says, “For I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed me, I was naked and you clothed me, I was sick and you visited me, I was in prison and you came to me… Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.”

Orphan Connect specifically focused on ministry to orphans and vulnerable children. Our aim is to provide holistic care and ministry addressing the spiritual, physical, economic, and educational needs of those we serve. Bringing Hope to the least of these is the calling we feel has been placed upon us as an organization, and it is in this segment of people to whom we are committed to serving.
